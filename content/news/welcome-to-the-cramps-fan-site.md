Welcome to the Cramps Fang Club. Whether you're a seasoned psychobilly traveler or a curious newcomer, you've found your home.

The Cramps were the greatest psychobilly band of all time — combining punk rock attitude with rockabilly swing, horror film aesthetics with raw garage energy. From the primitive punk of "I Got a Woman" to the swampy psychobilly of "Rock 'n' Roll Delirium", they carved out a sound that's both raw and theatrical.
