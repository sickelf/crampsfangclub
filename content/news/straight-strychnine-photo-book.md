Moonboy is taking pre-orders for [Straight Strychnine](https://moonboy.space/bookstore/the-cramps-straight-strychnine/), a limited photo book of The Cramps shot by Youri Lenquette between 1980 and 1991. It is the first book devoted entirely to photographs of the band, and most of the pictures have never been published.

The edition runs to 1,000 numbered copies, 224 pages, with more than 200 images. Lenny Kaye, whose *Nuggets* compilation was a formative influence on Lux and Ivy, writes the foreword. Will Hodgkinson, chief rock critic of *The Times*, writes the introduction, captions, and the interviews, with further contributions from Douglas Hart, Bobby Gillespie, and Pearl Harbour. The book also reprints three of Lenquette's original articles for the French magazine *Best*, translated into English.

Lenquette was there from the start of the band's European years. He photographed Lux Interior, Poison Ivy, Bryan Gregory, and Nick Knox launching their assault on Montpellier in April 1980, and he was still shooting them in 1991, when Slim Chance and Nickey Alexander were in the lineup. In between: Kid Congo Powers in his bone necklace, Lux baring all at the Eldorado in Paris in June 1984, and the band in full S&M drag for a 1987 session.

Four covers are up for pre-order, and every copy comes with two posters and two bookmarks:

- **Hardback, covers 1 and 2** — copies 1 to 500, £70. The first 323 come with a signed and numbered fine art print by Lenquette, sold only through Moonboy.
- **Softback, covers 3 and 4** — copies 501 to 1000, £43.

Postage and any local tax are extra, and Moonboy warns that international shipping is steep right now. This edition ships on 3 November 2026.

Pre-order at [moonboy.space](https://moonboy.space/bookstore/the-cramps-straight-strychnine/).
